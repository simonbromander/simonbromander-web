Shelfie support

Let’s get you back to your coffee.

Shelfie is a private coffee shelf and brew journal for iPhone. Here are quick answers to the most common questions.

Why is package scanning unavailable?

Live scanning requires a supported iPhone and camera permission. You can always choose a clear package photo instead. For the best result, photograph the front label in bright, even light.

Why did URL import miss a detail?

Coffee shops structure product pages differently. Shelfie fills every detail it can find and always lets you review the result before saving.

Where is my journal stored?

When iCloud is available, Shelfie stores your journal in your private iCloud container. Otherwise it remains on your device. Shelfie does not use its own account system or database.

How is freshness calculated?

Shelfie uses the roast date and the coffee's intended brew style to provide a practical freshness stage. Changing the roast date updates that guidance automatically.

How do I delete my data?

Delete coffees and logs inside Shelfie. Removing Shelfie deletes local app data; iCloud-synced data can be managed in your iCloud settings.

Still need help?

Email simon@bromander.se. Please include what you were trying to do and your iOS version. Do not include private coffee notes or photos unless they are necessary to explain the issue.
